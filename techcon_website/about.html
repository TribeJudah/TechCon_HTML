History

TechCon was established 8 years ago with the goal of connecting brilliant minds and fostering innovation within the global tech community. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted leader in tech event organization, consistently bringing together industry pioneers and emerging talents. Our commitment to advancing technology and knowledge sharing drives our mission to create meaningful connections worldwide. This legacy continues as we prepare to host our highly anticipated conference in Mumbai, welcoming tech enthusiasts from all corners of the globe.

Ralph Mupita

Ralph is a Zimbabwe-born engineer, businessman, and corporate exceutive, whose appointment as group president and chief executive of the MTN Group in September 2020. Prior to that, he served as the Chief financial officer for 3 years.
